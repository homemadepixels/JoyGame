# JoyGame
A 16 bit side scroller

## Big Ears Run

A bright, short platformer starring a black-and-tan pup with very big ears. There are two levels of five zones each. In every level she collects bones and teddy bears, sniffs out five hidden ferns, and hops into the car at the end. Pick a level on the title screen; finishing level 1 offers the next level.

**Play it:** open `index.html` in any web browser. It works with a keyboard on a laptop and with on-screen buttons on a phone.

### Level 1: Meadow

1. **Meadow** - grassy hills, bone blocks, beetles and a log to jump
2. **Toadstool Heights** - giant mushrooms, bounce blocks and bees
3. **Crystal Cave** - a dark tunnel with falling stone crushers
4. **Sky Bridge** - cloud platforms and moving lifts over a sea of clouds
5. **Sunset Run** - hopping frogs, a staircase, and the car waiting with its hatch open

### Level 2: Seaside

1. **Sunny Beach** - sand, palm trees, beach umbrellas and walking crabs
2. **Lagoon** - water fills the bottom of the screen. In the water she moves at half speed and sinks gently, with no other effects. Lily pads, rocks and small docks are low enough to jump onto from the lagoon floor.
3. **Jungle Canopy** - giant tree trunks, leafy platforms, frogs and a bounce block
4. **Moonlit Cliffs** - rocky ledges at night, moving lifts and one falling stone crusher
5. **Lantern Boardwalk** - a wooden boardwalk under the moon, frogs, a staircase, and the car

A fire hydrant at the start of each zone saves your spot.

### Things to collect

- **Bones** - float around the level and pop out of the paw-print blocks
- **Teddy bears** - she carries one in her mouth. Hold jump while falling to float down slowly. The bear also protects her from one hit.
- **Ferns** - five hidden in the level, one per zone. Stand on a fern and sniff to pick it up. They fill the meter in the top-right corner.

If she gets hit, all bones and teddy bears come back and the counts start over. Ferns you've found stay found.

### Controls

| | Keyboard | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Arrow keys or A / D | Left and right arrows |
| Jump | Space or Up (hold for higher) | JUMP (hold for higher) |
| Run | Shift | RUN (tap to switch on or off) |
| Sniff | S or Down (hold) | SNIFF (hold) |
| Pause | P | II |
| Sound | M | Music note |
